# Approved playtest changes — 2026-09-25

This records the user's two feedback batches and subsequent clarifications. The Master Design is preserved unchanged; these explicitly approved decisions supersede its affected Prototype 1 details.

- Menu input accepts synthesized touch clicks whose `isPrimary` is false; held Reel still enforces primary pointer ownership. Title art is a real image element, with one random day/night plate per load. This addresses the reported mobile menu failure and avoids reliance on a CSS background for the title plate.
- Settings exposes a vibration test and safely handles absent/rejected vibration APIs. Ordinary game haptics remain sparse event pulses. A browser accepting a request cannot confirm physical vibration.
- Remove rising strain audio and visible meter percentages; retain accessible meter values and discrete warnings. Landing uses softer, lower-frequency water noise with a gentle onset.
- Full Tension snaps immediately. At zero Catch Progress, leaving Reel released for three seconds loses the fish; show a warning after 1.5 seconds. Reeling cancels the countdown. Pause freezes it.
- Carp run Tension: correct 27 → 23/s, wrong/neutral 48 → 32/s. Pike: normal 25 → 22/s, run 34/55 → 27/38/s, surge 58 → 38/s. Keep progress rates, release recovery, move timing, Perch/Trout and Bass/Eel tuning unchanged pending further feedback.
- Directional prompts are large pulsing arrows. Reel follows a held horizontal drag within ±16% of the frame width, with direction measured from the initial press and input captured outside the button. Release centers it immediately.
- The seventh Book card is centered at the ordinary card width, with a restrained gold boss border.
- In-stage Settings orders navigation as Resume, Stage Select, Full Screen/Exit Full Screen, Title Screen. Title Settings has Reset Progress and Back instead of its duplicate fullscreen option. The main title fullscreen button remains.
- Reset one Stage or all Stages after a confirmation. Clear discoveries and encounter progression; preserve music/SFX/haptic preferences. Book immediately reflects the reset.
- Tapping the blurred backdrop dismisses Settings (or the resume prompt), resuming with input released. Picker/reset confirmations keep explicit action buttons.
- Center the gear icon. Blur only the background in Stage Select. The faint “Lake” entrance title lasts five seconds including fades, only on entry from Stage Select, never on returning from Book.
- Repeat-catch reveal retains the existing sequence; changing “New Book Entry” to “A fine catch” is accepted.

## Temporary testing feature

The Ready-only fish button below Settings opens a picker of all implemented Creatures in the current Stage, with names/art regardless of discovery. Confirm selects the next encounter and keeps it for later casts, retries and catches. Cancel discards the pending choice; Normal progression removes the override. The override survives Book visits but clears when leaving the Stage or reloading.

Test catches do not unlock Book entries or alter normal encounter progression. Marked `TEMPORARY PLAYTEST TOOL` in code/CSS for removal before the finished release. Wels Catfish is excluded until it has playable combat.

## Validation scope

Unit coverage includes immediate snapping, escaping, pause/cancel behavior, reset persistence, and six-Creature mechanics. Browser coverage exercises mouse and real emulated touch contacts, menu taps, complete catches, test-picker repetition/save isolation, bounded drag, settings/reset/Book flows, fullscreen/orientation, both title plates and the production `/arrantza/` path. WebKit and Firefox have separate compatibility smoke tests. Physical phone vibration, sound preference, and actual iOS browser behavior still need device confirmation.
